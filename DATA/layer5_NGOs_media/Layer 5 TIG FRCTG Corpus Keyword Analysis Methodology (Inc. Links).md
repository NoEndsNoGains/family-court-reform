# TIG/FRCTG Corpus (n=36) - Keyword Analysis Methodology

This folder contains the 36 primary source documents (meeting minutes) from the Transparency Implementation Group (TIG) and Financial Remedies Consultative & Transparency Group (FRCTG) analyzed in the paper.

A full-text keyword search was conducted across all 36 PDFs to quantify the internal reform groups' operational focus.

---

### Test 1: Publication Pathway Keywords (Expected Focus)

These keywords confirmed the overwhelming focus on media, reporting, and publishing.

* `media`: 29 hits
* `journalist`: 15 hits
* `publish`: 28 hits
* `judgment`: 18 hits
* `BAILII`: 4 hits
* **TOTAL:** 94 hits

---

### Test 2: Obtaining Pathway Keywords (Expected Silence)

These keywords tested for a focus on user-facing access barriers. Their systemic absence confirms the "Obtaining pathway" blind spot.

* `EX107`: **0 hits**
* `litigant access`: **0 hits**
* `cost`: **13 hits**
    * *Context:* All 13 hits referred to systemic/budgetary costs (e.g., the "cost of an anonymisation unit"), not the cost to a litigant.
* `transcript`: **3 hits**
    * *Context:* All 3 hits referred to the logistical burden of transcripts for *judges* (for publication), not the barriers for a litigant *obtaining* one.
